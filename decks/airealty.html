AIRealty

AI Copilot for Real Estate — Property DNA™ analysis, market intelligence and BUY/WATCH/PASS verdicts

✅ Production · v4.0 · 15 API endpoints

Problem

Property buyers and agents waste weeks manually comparing listings, estimating ROI and guessing market direction. AIRealty compresses this into minutes: paste a listing, get a data-driven verdict.

Key Modules

🧬 Property DNA™ 2.0

47 parameters across 7 categories — location, condition, infrastructure, legal risk, market dynamics, financials and hidden factors. Each property gets a complete genetic profile.

📊 Market Intelligence

Comparable sales, district price trends and demand analysis. Real data, no guesswork.

📈 ROI Calculator

Rental yield, appreciation scenarios and payback period — instant Monte Carlo simulation of investment outcomes.

🤖 AI Agent

Natural language chat about any property: "Is this flat a good deal for a 5-year hold?" — answers with evidence, not opinions.
