AILegal

AI Copilot for Legal Documents — ClauseDNA™ risk scoring, compliance scanning and contract intelligence

✅ Production · v4.0 · 18 API endpoints

Problem

Legal teams spend hours reviewing routine contracts. Most clauses are standard — but finding the one risky deviation takes an expert. AILegal scans contracts in seconds and flags what actually matters.

Key Modules

🧬 ClauseDNA™

50+ clause types with automatic risk scoring — indemnification, liability caps, termination rights, governing law and more. Each clause gets a severity rating.

🛡 Compliance Scanner

17 regulatory frameworks checked automatically. GDPR, data protection, mandatory clauses — all in one scan.

⚖️ Doc Comparison

Side-by-side diff of contract versions with risk-relevant change detection.

🤖 AI Agent

Ask legal questions in natural language: "What happens if we terminate early?" — answers cite the exact clause.
